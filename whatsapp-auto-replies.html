What it does

We connect your WhatsApp Business number to a system that instantly answers common customer questions — pricing, availability, hours, location, and more — in your own words and tone. If a question is too specific for the automation to handle, it's handed over to you or your team without the customer noticing any delay.

What's included

  • Automatic replies to your most common customer questions
  • A natural handoff to a real person when needed
  • Working hours settings so customers know when to expect a human reply
  • Basic broadcast messages to your existing customer list

How we set it up

  1. We go through your last month of WhatsApp chats to find your most repeated questions
  2. We write and test the automated replies in your own tone of voice
  3. We connect it to your existing WhatsApp Business number — no new number needed
  4. You approve it, and it goes live

Good fit for

Any business that gets repeated questions on WhatsApp — shops, salons, clinics, real estate agents, and service providers who currently reply manually and lose time (or leads) to slow responses.
