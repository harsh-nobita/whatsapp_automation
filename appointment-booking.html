What it does

We set up an automated booking system that lets customers pick an available slot themselves through WhatsApp or your website, get an instant confirmation, and receive a reminder before their appointment — cutting down no-shows and back-and-forth scheduling messages.

What's included

  • Self-service booking through WhatsApp or your website
  • Automatic confirmation once a slot is booked
  • Reminder messages before the appointment
  • Easy rescheduling without needing to call you

How we set it up

  1. We understand your current booking process and calendar setup
  2. We build the booking flow and connect it to your calendar
  3. We set up reminder timing that fits your business
  4. We test it with a few real bookings before going fully live

Good fit for

Salons, clinics, coaches, consultants, and any appointment-based business currently losing time to manual scheduling or no-shows.
