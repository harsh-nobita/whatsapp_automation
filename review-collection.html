Review collection

What it does

We set up an automated message that goes out shortly after a purchase or appointment, asking the customer for a quick review, with a direct link to your Google, Instagram, or other review page — and flags any negative feedback to you immediately, before it becomes a public review.

What's included

  • Automatic review request after every purchase or appointment
  • Direct link to your preferred review platform
  • Instant alert to you if feedback looks negative, before it's posted publicly
  • Simple tracking of how many requests turn into reviews

How we set it up

  1. We connect it to the point where a purchase or appointment is marked complete
  2. We write the request message in your tone, with your review link
  3. We set up the negative-feedback alert so you can respond privately first
  4. We test the full flow with a few real customers before rollout

Good fit for

Any business that depends on online reviews to win new customers — restaurants, salons, clinics, and local service businesses.
