What it does

We connect your lead sources — ads, forms, WhatsApp, or your website — to an automated system that immediately reaches out to new enquiries, asks a few qualifying questions, and either books them in or flags them for your sales team to follow up personally.

What's included

  • Instant automatic reply to every new enquiry
  • Basic qualifying questions to understand what they need
  • Leads organized and routed to you or your team
  • Follow-up reminders for leads that go quiet

How we set it up

  1. We connect to wherever your leads currently come from (ads, forms, WhatsApp)
  2. We set up the instant response and qualifying questions
  3. We decide with you how leads should be routed or prioritized
  4. We test it with real incoming leads before full rollout

Good fit for

Businesses running ads or getting online enquiries — real estate, education, coaching, and B2B services where speed of follow-up directly affects sales.
