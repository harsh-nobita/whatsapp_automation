What it does

We add a chat assistant to your website that's trained on your own products, services, and FAQs. It answers visitor questions in real time and can guide them toward booking, buying, or contacting you — even outside your business hours.

What's included

  • A chat widget added to your website
  • Trained on your actual products, services, and FAQs
  • Option to collect a visitor's contact details before ending the chat
  • Works on both desktop and mobile

How we set it up

  1. We review your website content, FAQs, and any documents you'd like it trained on
  2. We build and test the chat assistant against real visitor questions
  3. We add it to your website with a short code snippet
  4. We refine it based on the questions it actually receives

Good fit for

Businesses with a website that gets traffic but few direct enquiries — e-commerce stores, service businesses, and course or consultancy websites.
