What it does

We set up an AI voice assistant that answers your business phone line, has a natural back-and-forth conversation with callers, answers frequently asked questions, and can book or reschedule appointments directly into your calendar — all without a human picking up.

What's included

  • A natural-sounding voice assistant for inbound calls
  • Appointment booking directly into your existing calendar
  • Call summaries sent to you after every call
  • Escalation to a real person for anything it can't handle

How we set it up

  1. We map out the calls you get most often and what callers usually need
  2. We build and test the voice assistant on real call scenarios
  3. We connect it to your business phone number and calendar
  4. We monitor the first few weeks of calls and fine-tune it

Good fit for

Clinics, salons, real estate agents, auto shops, and any business that loses bookings to missed or unanswered calls, especially outside business hours.
